## Video 1 — Website redesign (core web development; Performance & UX / business websites)
- **Primary keyword:** does my website need a redesign
- **Secondary:** signs your website needs a redesign · website redesign vs rebuild · website refresh vs redesign · website redesign for small business · keep SEO during website redesign (301 redirects)
- **Intent:** problem-aware business owners checking whether their site is "bad enough" to redo, then deciding how far to go.
- **Audience problem:** the site looks dated, is awkward on phones, or needs a developer for every small edit; they're unsure whether a cosmetic refresh is enough or a rebuild is needed, and worry about losing Google rankings.
- **Angle:** three plain signs → refresh only changes the look, a rebuild fixes structure/speed/mobile/features → either way map pages and redirect old links.
- **Why now:** many "signs your website needs a redesign in 2026" articles published this year; redesign-vs-rebuild comparison pages are a common format (content gap for short, plain-language video).
- **Sources:** [lowcode.agency – signs](https://www.lowcode.agency/blog/signs-your-website-needs-a-redesign), [Volusion – Start 2026 strong](https://volusion.com/blog/start-2026-strong-website-redesign/), [kijo.london – when to rebuild](https://kijo.london/blog/website-redesign), [webfactory – how often to redesign](https://www.webfactory.co.uk/blog-articles/how-often-should-you-redesign-your-website-signs-its-time-for-an-update), [Digital Nexa – redesign vs rebuild](https://blog.digitalnexa.com/website-redesign-vs-website-rebuild-which-actually-need), [Lean Labs – redesign, rebuild or replatform](https://www.leanlabs.com/faq/b2b-websites/how-do-you-decide-between-redesigning-rebuilding-or-replatforming-a-website), [informationexperts – redesign vs refresh](https://informationexperts.com/2025/09/23/how-to-decide-when-your-website-needs-a-redesign-vs-a-refresh/)

## Video 2 — Restaurant table reservations on your own website (Booking & reservations; restaurants & hospitality)
- **Primary keyword:** restaurant reservation system for website
- **Secondary:** online table booking for restaurants · how to add online reservations to restaurant website · restaurant booking system with reminders · reduce restaurant no-shows · restaurant waitlist system
- **Intent:** restaurant/café owners looking to take bookings online instead of by phone; comparing plug-in tools with a booking flow on their own site.
- **Audience problem:** phone bookings interrupt service; no single view of the night; no-shows; guest notes (allergies, occasions) lost.
- **Angle:** guest flow (pick time → instant confirmation → reminder) → staff view of the night with wait list and guest notes → connected to website, payments, email, reports via APIs. No claims about commissions, no-show reduction figures or named third-party programs.
- **Why now:** reservation software vendors and WordPress guides actively publish "add reservations to your website" content ahead of the holiday season.
- **Sources:** [UpMenu – restaurant reservation system](https://www.upmenu.com/restaurant-reservation-system/), [UpMenu – managing reservations](https://www.upmenu.com/blog/how-to-manage-reservations-in-restaurant-handle-customers/), [Tablein](https://tablein.com/), [SimplyBook – table booking](https://simplybook.me/online-scheduler-for-events-and-entertainment-services/table-booking-system-for-restaurants), [WPBeginner – reservation system in WordPress](https://www.wpbeginner.com/plugins/how-to-add-a-restaurant-reservation-system-in-wordpress/), [Loop – add reservations to restaurant website](https://loop.fans/blog/add-online-reservations-to-restaurant-website), [Eat App – setting up online reservations](https://restaurant.eatapp.co/knowledge/setting-up-online-reservations)
