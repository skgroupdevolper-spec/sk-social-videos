## Video 1 — WordPress (page builder vs custom theme)
- Primary keyword: **page builder vs custom WordPress theme**
- Secondary: custom WordPress theme development · Elementor vs custom theme · WordPress page builder performance · custom Gutenberg blocks · WordPress developer for business
- Search intent: commercial investigation (owner whose builder site feels heavy or hard to manage, weighing a rebuild).
- Audience problem: builder sites carry extra nested markup and load CSS/JS for unused features; layouts tied to one plugin; editors can break the brand; updates need testing.
- Angle: balanced. A page builder was a good start and is still fine for short campaigns, quick first launches and teams who change layouts constantly; a custom theme with custom blocks ships only the code a site needs while editors keep editing content. Then how SK Developer would move a site across.
- Why now: several "custom WordPress vs page builders 2026" articles (WPBrigade, WPRiders, FatLab, Harmonic Design, gautamkhorana.com) are ranking; the question is active.
- Sources:
  - https://fatlabwebsupport.com/blog/wordpress-development/page-builders-vs-custom-themes/ (fetched: nested markup, unused CSS/JS, serialized data lock-in, when a builder is acceptable)
  - https://wpbrigade.com/custom-wordpress-development-vs-page-builders/ (fetched: when each is right; builders suit landing pages, MVPs, frequent layout edits)
  - https://harmonicdesign.ca/page-builders-vs-custom-wordpress-themes-the-real-performance-cost/ (fetch blocked by bot check; title only)
  - https://wpriders.com/custom-themes-vs-page-builders-better-roi-2026/
  - https://savvy.co.il/en/blog/marketing/custom-wordpress-theme-vs-page-builders/
- No statistics used. FatLab's DOM-size, PageSpeed and 5-year cost tables were **not** used: no method given, written by an agency selling custom builds.

## Video 2 — Booking & reservations (custom booking platform)
- Primary keyword: **custom booking system for small business**
- Secondary: online appointment booking system · salon booking software · clinic appointment booking system · booking platform development · appointment reminders
- Search intent: commercial investigation (service business taking bookings by phone/DMs, comparing a generic app with its own platform).
- Audience problem: bookings scattered across phone, messages and paper; double bookings; no reminders; generic apps with fixed rules/fields, per-seat pricing that grows, vendor branding, export limits.
- Angle: the customer's side (pick service → free slot → reminder), the team's side (concept booking dashboard: staff schedules, deposits and cancellation rules, client history), generic app vs custom platform (balanced: generic apps are a good start), who it's for.
- Why now: many 2026 salon/booking software roundups and no-code booking guides; custom-build pages describe the gaps listed above.
- Sources:
  - https://www.raftlabs.com/industries/salon-barbershop/appointment-booking-software (fetched: off-the-shelf limits — rigid rules, per-seat cost growth, limited integrations, export lock-in; custom features — deposits/cancellation rules, multi-touch reminders, waitlists, staff calendars)
  - https://happoin.com/en-gb/salon-booking-software
  - https://www.zenoti.com/en-uk/salon-management-software/salon-booking-software
  - https://www.nxcode.io/ru/resources/news/build-booking-scheduling-app-no-code-2026
- No statistics used (no no-show reduction figures claimed).
