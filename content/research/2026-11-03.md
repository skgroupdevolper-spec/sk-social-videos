# Keyword research — 2026-11-03

Method: WebSearch (3 queries) + WebFetch of 3 pages. No keyword tool access, so **search volume, difficulty and trend data are unverified**. Keywords were chosen from phrases that recur in page titles and community questions, not from measured volume.

## Video 1 — Business management systems (custom CRM)
- Primary keyword: **custom CRM for small business**
- Secondary: outgrown spreadsheets · CRM vs spreadsheets · custom business management software · custom CRM development · replace Excel with CRM
- Search intent: commercial investigation (owner suspects spreadsheets are holding the team back, comparing options).
- Audience problem: data spread across many sheets, missed follow-ups, time lost copying data between files, sheets that slow down as they grow, no single view of the business.
- Angle: "5 signs you've outgrown spreadsheets" reframed as a short self-check, then spreadsheet vs custom system, a labelled concept dashboard and how SK Developer would plan it.
- Why now: many vendors and agencies published "outgrown spreadsheets / custom CRM in 2026" content (Nimble, Salesforce, Bitrix24, Prialto, Solguruz 2026 cost guide) — the question is active, and most content pushes off-the-shelf CRMs, leaving room for a "built around how you work" angle.
- Sources:
  - https://www.prialto.com/blog/5-signs-your-business-has-outgrown-spreadsheets-and-should-use-crm-best-practices (fetched: scattered data, lost prospects, time spent organising, sheets slowing/crashing)
  - https://www.nimble.com/blog/crm-vs-spreadsheets/
  - https://www.salesforce.com/blog/small-business/spreadsheet-vs-crm/
  - https://www.infomazeelite.com/blog/five-signs-youve-outgrown-excel-and-need-a-crm/

  - https://dev.to/arobit_digital/custom-crm-development-vs-ready-made-crm-which-one-should-you-choose-2b68
- No statistics used in the video.

## Video 2 — Shopify (custom theme sections vs app bloat)
- Primary keyword: **Shopify app bloat**
- Secondary: Shopify custom sections · custom Shopify theme development · Shopify apps vs custom code · Shopify Liquid sections · Shopify developer
- Search intent: informational → commercial (store owner wonders whether to install yet another app or have a section built).
- Audience problem: each new app adds fees, scripts and settings; overlapping apps; harder-to-manage store.
- Angle: many "app" needs are presentation (FAQ, comparison table, trust badges near the buy button, value row, campaign landing block) and fit a focused custom theme section; apps still make sense for features with their own data/workflow (reviews, subscriptions, loyalty, returns). Balanced, not anti-app.
- Why now: March 2026 article "Shopify without app bloat" and a recent Shopify Community thread asking apps vs custom tweaks; several "section store" apps rank for the same queries.
- Sources:
  - https://topwebworks.com/blog/shopify-without-app-bloat (fetched: hidden costs of apps, sections for presentation needs, apps for systems with data/workflow, audit regularly)
  - https://community.shopify.com/t/s-it-better-to-rely-on-shopify-apps-or-custom-tweaks-for-store-optimization/587108 (fetched: lean setup, Liquid sections for simple changes, apps for ongoing needs; note theme edits need care on theme upgrades)
  - https://community.shopify.com/t/theme-sections-and-best-practice/252386
  - https://meetanshi.com/blog/best-shopify-section-store-apps/
- No statistics used in the video (the community "5–10 apps / 20+ scripts" remarks are opinions and were not used).
