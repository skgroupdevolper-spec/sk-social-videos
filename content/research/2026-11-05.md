## Video 1 — APIs & integrations (educational)
- Primary keyword: **API integration for small business**
- Secondary: eliminate double data entry · connect business apps · CRM and accounting integration · webhook automation · Shopify / WooCommerce integration
- Search intent: informational → commercial investigation (owner whose team retypes orders, customers and invoices between tools).
- Audience problem: store, CRM, accounting, shipping and spreadsheets don't share data; the same details are entered again and again; mistakes and delays; nobody trusts which system is right.
- Angle: "the join between your tools is a person" — show the copy-paste loop, then how an API/webhook integration moves one event (a new order) into every system automatically; what to connect first; balanced note that no-code connectors suit simple flows, custom integration suits your own rules and data.
- Why now: active 2026 guides on API integration for SMEs and "eliminate double data entry" pages; government small-business training (business.gov.au) runs an integration basics session.
- Sources:
  - https://business.gov.au/events-and-training/making-your-systems-talk-to-each-other-integration-basics (fetched: disconnected tools mean "entering the same information again and again", which "quietly eats into your week"; no-code connectors as one option; decide which double entry to remove first)

## Video 2 — AI chatbots & automation (capability)
- Primary keyword: **AI customer support chatbot for small business**
- Secondary: custom AI chatbot · AI chatbot for website · WhatsApp AI chatbot · chatbot with human handoff · AI chatbot trained on your business data
- Search intent: commercial investigation (business owner getting the same customer questions all day and after hours, comparing a generic bot with a custom one).
- Audience problem: repeat questions (hours, prices, order status, bookings) answered by hand; replies are slow after hours; generic bots give vague answers because they don't know the business and can't reach its systems.
- Angle: concept chat showing a grounded answer and a clean hand-off to a person; what a good assistant should and shouldn't do; generic vs custom (balanced: off-the-shelf suits simple FAQs); how SK Developer builds one (your content → connect your systems → test with real questions → launch with handoff).
- Why now: many 2026 "best AI chatbot for small business" and "WhatsApp AI customer support 2026" guides; custom vs off-the-shelf comparisons ranking.
