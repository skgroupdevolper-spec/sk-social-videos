Method: WebSearch (US results) and WebFetch of ranking pages. No keyword tool was available, so **all search volumes are unverified**. Choices are based on how often a phrase appears as a page title or question across results.

## Video 1: Landing page not converting (pillar: Performance & UX / business websites)
- **Primary keyword:** landing page not converting (volume unverified)
- **Secondary:** why is my landing page not converting · website not getting inquiries · landing page optimization · mobile landing page design · landing page developer
- **Intent:** problem-solving / commercial. Owners are paying for traffic (ads, social, SEO) and get visits without leads.
- **Audience problem:** visitors arrive and leave without contacting them. They don't know if the cause is the traffic, the design or the page itself.
- **Angle:** four common leaks you can check today (vague headline, slow mobile page, long form, generic button), then the fix: one page, one clear action, a short form and a fast mobile build. SK Developer builds landing pages in WordPress, Shopify or custom code.
- **Why now:** the phrase "landing page not converting" is the title of many recent guides (Zoho, Landingi, HawkSEM, ATJ, a May 2026 blog post), which suggests ongoing demand (unverified). It also fits the 11-05 brief's "landing pages that convert" focus.
- **Facts used (qualitative only):** unclear value proposition, long forms that ask too much too soon, slow loading, generic CTA text like "Submit", and poor mobile design are commonly listed causes (Zoho). Zoho's "3 seconds / more than half leave" figure has no cited source, so it was **not** used. No conversion rates or stats appear in the video.

## Video 2: Customer portal for small business (pillar: Dashboards / customer portals; custom development)
- **Primary keyword:** customer portal development (volume unverified)
- **Secondary:** client portal for small business · custom customer portal · self-service customer portal · client portal vs saas · customer dashboard development
- **Intent:** commercial investigation. Service businesses, B2B suppliers and agencies want clients to find invoices, order status and documents without emailing.
- **Audience problem:** the team answers the same "where's my invoice / any update / resend that file" messages every day, and client files are scattered across email.
- **Angle:** a secure login per client (invoices, order status, documents, bookings, messages), where each client sees only their own data, synced from the systems the business already uses. Built with React.js, Next.js, Node.js, PHP and REST APIs, with role-based access.
- **Why now:** many 2026-dated "customer portal software" roundups and "custom client portal vs SaaS" articles are ranking (Supportbench, Clinked, Kolonell), so the topic looks current (unverified).
- **Facts used (qualitative only):** a customer portal is a login-protected, self-service space where each customer sees only their own account, documents and history. Typical features are invoices, documents, order status, tickets and messaging. The benefits are a lighter support workload and information kept in one secure place (Clinked). Clinked's third-party percentages (Gartner etc.) were **not** used. Kolonell cost figures were not used.
