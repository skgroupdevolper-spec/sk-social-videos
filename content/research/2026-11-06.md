# Keyword research — 2026-11-06

Method: WebSearch (US results) and WebFetch of ranking pages, plus forum/community roundups (Reddit summaries, Shopify Community). There was no keyword tool, so **all search volumes are unverified**. Choices are based on how often a phrase shows up as a page title or question across results, not on measured volume.

## Video 1: Shopify vs WooCommerce (pillar: eCommerce, Shopify + WooCommerce/WordPress)
- **Primary keyword:** shopify vs woocommerce (volume unverified)
- **Secondary:** woocommerce vs shopify for small business · shopify or woocommerce which should I choose · best ecommerce platform for small business · woocommerce developer · shopify developer
- **Intent:** commercial investigation. Someone about to launch or move an online store wants to know which platform fits them.
- **Audience problem:** they get conflicting advice, worry about choosing wrong and paying to migrate later, and don't know what "hosting", "plugins" or "transaction fees" mean for them in practice.
- **Angle:** neither platform "wins". Ask three questions (how fast you need to launch, how much control and customisation you need, who will maintain it) and the answer follows. SK Developer builds on both.
- **Why now:** many "Shopify vs WooCommerce 2026" comparisons are being published, and the phrase keeps appearing as a question on Reddit and in the Shopify Community, so demand looks current (unverified).
- **Facts used (qualitative only, no prices or stats):** Shopify is a hosted, all-in-one platform that is quick to set up and has more features built in. WooCommerce is an open-source plugin for WordPress: you provide hosting, and you get more control and flexibility in exchange for more setup and maintenance. Startups.co.uk says Shopify suits most small businesses that want a fast launch, and WooCommerce suits niche needs, existing WordPress sites and teams that can maintain it.

  - https://community.shopify.com/t/i-am-confused-between-shopify-and-wocommerce/89855

  - https://dev.to/amanhstu/woocommerce-vs-shopify-which-is-better-for-us-small-businesses-in-2026-5apo

## Video 2: Custom web app vs another SaaS subscription (pillar: custom web applications)
- **Primary keyword:** custom software vs saas (volume unverified)
- **Secondary:** build vs buy software · when to build a custom web app · custom web application development · replace saas with custom software · custom web app for small business
- **Intent:** informational/commercial. Owners juggling many subscriptions and workarounds want to know when a custom build is worth it.
- **Audience problem:** they pay for several tools that each do part of the job, stitch them together with exports and copy-paste, and their real workflow still doesn't fit.
- **Angle:** be honest. Keep SaaS for commodity jobs (accounting, email, calendar). Build custom only where your process is your edge, the tools fight how you work, or workarounds eat your team's week. Then show how SK Developer runs a custom build: discovery, then a focused first version (MVP), then grow.
- **Why now:** "build vs buy" articles dated 2026 rank for this query (for example, rockingtech.co.uk, April 2026). Subscription sprawl is a recurring theme.
- **Facts used (qualitative only):** a hybrid stack is usually best and commodity tools stay SaaS. Build when the process is a real differentiator, when you can maintain the software, and when your need for change outruns the vendor's roadmap. Mature categories (CRM, accounting, booking) often suit SaaS. The source sells custom builds, so its numbers were **not** used. No costs or savings figures appear in the video.
- **Sources:**

  - https://eleks.com/blog/saas-vs-custom-software/

  - https://albrightlabs.com/blog/build-vs-buy-when-a-custom-app-beats-a-saas-subscription
