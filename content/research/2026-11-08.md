# Keyword research — 2026-11-08

Method: WebSearch (US results) and WebFetch of ranking pages. No keyword tool was available, so **all search volumes are unverified**. Phrases were chosen because they recur as page titles and questions across results.

## Video 1: WordPress maintenance (pillar: WordPress)
- **Primary keyword:** WordPress maintenance checklist (volume unverified)
- **Secondary:** WordPress website maintenance · WordPress maintenance for small business · outdated WordPress plugins · WordPress backup and security · WordPress care plan
- **Intent:** informational → commercial. Owners whose site was built once and then left alone want to know what upkeep it needs and who should do it.
- **Audience problem:** nobody has updated plugins, checked backups or tested the contact form in months. They only find out when something breaks.
- **Angle:** a simple rhythm. Weekly: updates, a backup check, test the forms. Monthly: core update on staging, malware scan, speed check. Quarterly: remove unused plugins, rotate passwords, do a restore test. SK Developer handles WordPress maintenance, speed and security.
- **Why now:** many "WordPress maintenance checklist 2026" and "WordPress security in 2026" guides rank (fs-code, Seahawk, Stark Media, wbcom "vulnerability wave 2026"), so interest looks steady (unverified). WordPress has been off the schedule since 11-04.
- **Facts used (qualitative only):** weekly/monthly/quarterly task lists (confirm off-site backups, plugin/theme updates, test forms/checkout, Site Health; monthly core update via staging, malware scan, SSL expiry, PageSpeed; quarterly plugin audit, credential rotation, restore drill) from fs-code. Its thresholds (48 h, 12 months, etc.) are rules of thumb with no cited source, so they were **not** shown as statistics. WP-CLI commands in the code scene (`wp core check-update`, `wp plugin list --update=available`, `wp db export`) are standard WP-CLI and labelled illustrative.
- **Sources:**

  - https://wpremote.com/wordpress-maintenance-checklist/

## Video 2: Custom inventory / stock management system (pillar: Business management systems — ERP/inventory; custom development)
- **Primary keyword:** inventory management system for small business (volume unverified)
- **Secondary:** custom inventory management system · spreadsheet vs inventory software · real-time stock tracking · multi-location inventory · small business ERP
- **Intent:** commercial investigation. Product sellers and wholesalers with stock in more than one place want counts they can trust.
- **Audience problem:** the sheet says one number, the shelf says another. They oversell online, run out without warning and spend hours reconciling.
- **Angle:** one live stock system: every sale, return and delivery updates stock in real time across the store, warehouse and website, with low-stock alerts, purchase orders and reports. Connected to Shopify/WooCommerce and accounting through APIs. Different from 11-03 (CRM for customers and sales): this one is about **stock accuracy**.
- **Why now:** "spreadsheet vs inventory software / ERP" pages dated 2025–2026 rank widely (Capterra, Versa Cloud ERP, ACCEO, a 2026 DEV post), so the topic looks current (unverified).
- **Facts used (qualitative only):** spreadsheets aren't built for real-time updates, so overselling and surprise stockouts follow; multiple locations and channels break visibility; real-time stock, multi-location visibility, reorder rules and reporting are the core features (Capterra). Capterra's buyer percentages come from its own buyer calls and were **not** used.
